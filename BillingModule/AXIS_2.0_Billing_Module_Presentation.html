🔥 The Seven Billing Failures We Face Daily
1
Duplicate Loads
Multi-person entry, clone misuse, driver self-entry in TMF/Precast create duplicate invoices.
2
Wrong BOL/Tickets
Driver uploads wrong document, or correct BOL attached to wrong load. No validation catches it.
3
Wrong Rates
Rates live in sticky notes, emails, and heads—not the system. Changes never get updated.
4
FSC Errors
Manual updates fall behind. FSC missing, duplicated, or incorrectly calculated.
5
Missing Accessorials
Detention, driver assist, and other charges not reported or not entered. Revenue leakage.
6
Missing Stop-Offs
TMF drivers self-enter loads, skip extra stops. Dispatch rubber-stamps without checking.
7
Weight Mismatches
Load weight doesn't match BOL for weight-billed fleets. Direct revenue impact.
😰 Current Process: A Day in the Life
Step 1: Driver Uploads BOL
Driver completes delivery and uploads BOL via app. Load auto-moves to "Delivered" status.
System has no idea if document is correct, legible, or even related to this load.
Step 2: First Validation (Dispatch)
4 people manually review each load—checking BOL, rate, weight, locations, stops, internal comments for accessorials.
With hundreds of loads per day, corners get cut. Loads get rubber-stamped.
Step 3: Second Validation (Billing)
3 more people review the same things AGAIN, plus FSC validation and rate hunting through sticky notes and emails.
Pure duplication of effort. Two teams checking the same things because neither trusts the other's work.
Step 4: Dual System Transfer
Data transferred from AXIS 1.0 (operations) to McLeod LoadMaster (billing).
Transcription errors, duplicate entries, data loss. Pure chaos.
Step 5: Invoice Generation
Invoice generated in McLeod, enters rendition queue based on customer preferences.
Step 6: Wait for Disputes
Invoices go out via email. And then we wait to see which ones come back as disputes.
500-750 problem invoices per week. Credits, rebills, apologies, damaged relationships.
💔 The Human Cost

Staff Burnout: 7 people spend their days chasing errors instead of adding value.

Retention Risk: High-performers don't want to spend their careers fixing preventable mistakes.

Goal Distraction: Instead of growth and customer relationships, the team is trapped in an endless cycle of credits and rebills.

Customer Trust: Every incorrect invoice chips away at our reputation.

Three-Gate Validation System
Systematic validation replaces "double-check and hope"
🎯 Core Principle

Every load must pass through three validation gates before an invoice is generated. Failures at any gate block the load from progressing until resolved. No more rubber-stamping. No more hoping someone else catches the error.

1
Pre-Dispatch Validation
Before a load can be dispatched
🛑
Rate exists — Must be in customer profile (no sticky notes)
🛑
Rate approved — Customer approval on file
🛑
Rate not expired — Within effective date range
🛑
FSC configured — Profile defined (applies/doesn't/all-in)
🛑
Customer credit OK — Not on credit hold
✅ Eliminates: Wrong rates, missing FSC configuration, rate hunting through emails/sticky notes
2
Delivery Validation
When driver uploads BOL and marks delivered
⚠️
BOL # extraction — OCR extracts ticket # from document
🛑
BOL # match — Extracted # compared to load record
⚠️
Weight extraction — OCR extracts weight from BOL
🛑
Weight match — For weight-sensitive fleets only
⚠️
Stop count match — Stops on BOL vs. stops entered
✅ Eliminates: Wrong BOL uploaded, weight mismatches, missing stop-offs
3
Pre-Billing Validation
Before invoice generation
🛑
All fields complete — Rate, weight, locations, references
🛑
BOL attached — Legible POD document uploaded
🛑
FSC applied correctly — Calculated per customer profile
🛑
Accessorials complete — Applied or waived with reason
🛑
Rate verified — Matches profile for pickup date
✅ Eliminates: Missing accessorials, FSC errors, incomplete loads reaching billing
💰
Rate Engine
No more sticky notes. No more email hunting. No more guessing.
🎯 Core Principle

Rates must exist in the system with customer approval before any load can be dispatched. Rates are locked at pickup date and cannot be changed without Level 2 Manager approval.

📋 Rate Rules
Rule Behavior
Rate Anchor Rate locked based on Pickup Date (not creation date)
Rate Lock Once load picks up, rate won't change even if rate file updates
Rate Expiration 1 Year from effective date — forces annual audit
Rate Renewal Manual renewal required unless flagged as auto-renew
Rate Override Level 2 Manager approval required after pickup
Rate Activation Requires Customer Approval (or Level 3 override)

• Rate flagged as "Pending Customer Confirmation"
• Customer approval still required within defined period or rate expires

FSC Engine
Fully configurable, auto-calculating, audit-trailed
🎯 Core Principle

Every customer can have a completely unique FSC configuration. FSC files are versioned (never deleted), auto-calculate from index sources, and require customer approval for material changes.

📝 FSC File Versioning

FSC files can be edited but NEVER deleted.

• Every edit logged with: Who, When, What changed (old/new value), Why
• Files have Start Date and End Date for version control
• Historical loads use the FSC file that was active on their effective date
• Full audit trail preserved forever
